# M2 — Observations and follow-ups

Goal: a teacher records an observation in ≤ 15 s; follow-ups and needs-attention work.

---

### M2-01 Entries domain
**Lane** Feature A · **Depends** M0-06 · **Spec** [02-schema § Timeline, Entry details](../docs/architecture/02-schema.md#timeline), [03 § Rules](../docs/architecture/03-auth-and-access.md#rules-beyond-the-matrix)

- `server/entries/categories.ts` (Zod registry); actions: create (single and group with `group_key`), edit, soft-delete (24-hour own rule), close, reopen, add follow-up (child entry with `parent_id`); queries: by child, by id with thread.
- Restricted filtering in every query.

**Accept**
- [ ] All "Entries" rows in [06-testing](../docs/architecture/06-testing.md#required-tests-by-area) are covered
- [ ] Teacher queries never return restricted entries

### M2-02 Quick observation screen
**Lane** Feature A · **Depends** M2-01, M0-08 · **Spec** [04-ui-spec § Quick observation](../docs/architecture/04-ui-spec.md#quick-observation-observe-target--15-s-d23)

- `ChildPicker`, `ChipGroup`, `FollowUpChips`; category-specific fields; sticky Save; toast with Undo.

**Accept**
- [ ] E2E: record an observation with 2 tags and a 1-week follow-up in ≤ 6 taps + note
- [ ] Group save for 5 children creates 5 rows with one `group_key`
- [ ] Opening from a profile pre-selects that child

### M2-03 Journey tab + timeline view
**Lane** Feature B · **Depends** M2-01 · **Spec** [02-schema § Views](../docs/architecture/02-schema.md#views-and-functions), [04-ui-spec § Child profile](../docs/architecture/04-ui-spec.md#child-profile-childrenid)

**Accept**
