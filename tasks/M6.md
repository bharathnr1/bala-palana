# M6 — Health, documents, family, skills and achievements

---

### M6-01 Health
**Lane** Feature A · **Depends** M2-01 · **Spec** D13, [03 § Rules](../docs/architecture/03-auth-and-access.md#rules-beyond-the-matrix)

- The Health tab: profile form (`health.write`), health entries list; `AlertBanner` shows `medical_alerts` to all staff.

**Accept**
- [ ] A teacher sees medical alerts in the header but no Health tab content or health entries (server test)

### M6-02 Documents
**Lane** Feature B · **Depends** M1-01 · **Spec** [02-schema § Files](../docs/architecture/02-schema.md#files)

- The Documents tab: upload (camera or file; compressed images), list by kind, view via signed URL, soft delete, set as profile photo; restricted default from kind.

**Accept**
- [ ] A teacher can't list, view or upload restricted kinds (server test)
- [ ] Signed URLs are generated per view; no public URLs anywhere

### M6-03 Family and education history
**Lane** Feature A · **Depends** M0-08

- The Family tab: guardians (occupation, skills, interests, seva involvement), add an existing guardian (for siblings), siblings list derived from shared guardians; the education history section.

**Accept**
- [ ] Linking an existing guardian to a second child shows them as siblings on both profiles

### M6-04 Skills and achievements
**Lane** Feature B · **Depends** M2-02, M6-02

- The Skills & achievements tab: skills by area, achievements with level and result, a certificate link; `v_achievements`.

**Accept**
- [ ] Recording an achievement through quick observation with a certificate shows on the tab and in the Journey

### M6-05 Role and privacy tests
**Lane** QA · **Depends** M6-01, M6-02

- E2E for each role's visibility of health and restricted documents.

**Accept**
- [ ] Green in CI
