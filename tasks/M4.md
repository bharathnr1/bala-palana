# M4 — Academics

Goal: marks, grades or remarks per term; an academics tab; a grade summary.

---

### M4-01 Assessments domain
**Lane** Feature A · **Depends** M0-04 · **Spec** [02-schema § Academics](../docs/architecture/02-schema.md#academics-and-health), D10

- Upsert (unique per child/year/term/subject), delete, queries by child and by grade/term/subject; add assessments to `v_child_timeline`.

**Accept**
- [ ] The Academics rows in [06-testing](../docs/architecture/06-testing.md#required-tests-by-area) are covered

### M4-02 Bulk entry
**Lane** Feature A · **Depends** M4-01 · **Spec** [04-ui-spec § Academics](../docs/architecture/04-ui-spec.md#academics-academics)

- Pick year/grade/term/subject → rows per child; marks + max (max remembered per subject/term), or a grade letter, or a remark; autosave per row with a saved indicator.

**Accept**
- [ ] Entering 30 children's marks needs no page reload; invalid marks show inline errors

### M4-03 Academics tab
**Lane** Feature B · **Depends** M4-01, M2-01

- Per academic year: a term × subject table, a percentage trend sparkline per subject, and open concerns by subject (from entries).

**Accept**
- [ ] A child with assessments in 2 years shows both, grouped

### M4-04 Grade summary report
**Lane** Feature B · **Depends** M4-01 · **Spec** [design §7](../docs/design-doc.md#7-reports)

- `v_grade_subject_summary`; a `/reports/grade-summary` page with filters, chart, table and CSV export (the generic exporter comes in M8-03; a simple CSV route is fine here and gets refactored later).

**Accept**
- [ ] Averages match a hand-computed fixture
